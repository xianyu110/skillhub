# Lighting recipes

- **soft-window**: large soft key from the left, gentle falloff, pale shadow — calm, natural, wellness.
- **hard-sun**: crisp directional sunlight, long hard shadow, high contrast — summer, outdoor, energetic.
- **studio-rim**: dark set, strong rim light outlining the silhouette, subtle reflection on glossy floor — premium tech, spirits.
- **pastel-pop**: flat even light, saturated pastel set, colored shadow — playful, Gen-Z, beauty.
- **warm-dusk**: low warm key, cool fill, haze — cozy, home, coffee.
