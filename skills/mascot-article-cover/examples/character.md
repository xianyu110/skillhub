## Character bible: Momo

- Who: a small round paper fox, curious and gentle.
- Colors: body #F2A65A, accent #FFF4E0, outline #3B2F2F.
- Always wears/has: a tiny teal scarf (#2A9D8F).
- Proportions: big head, short legs, oversized fluffy tail, about 2.5 heads tall.
- Style: clean slightly wobbly ink outline, flat gouache-like fills with soft paper grain.
- Never: shows teeth; more than one character in the scene.
